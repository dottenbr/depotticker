Privacy

Depotticker has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer receives no data from the app.

What stays on your devices

Your portfolios, positions and purchase prices are stored on your device. In the App Store versions they are also stored in your own iCloud account so that your devices stay in step; only you can read them.

What is sent over the network

To show prices, charts, key figures and news, the app requests data for the symbols in your portfolios from Yahoo Finance. These requests contain the symbols and your IP address, as any web request does. They do not contain quantities, purchase prices or anything that identifies you.

The direct-download Mac version also fetches a small update file from this website to see whether a newer version exists.

AI agents

The optional MCP server gives an assistant that you connect yourself access to your portfolio data on your Mac. What that assistant does with the data is governed by its own privacy terms.
