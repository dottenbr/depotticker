Privacy

Depotticker has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer receives no data from the app.

What stays on your devices

Your portfolios, positions and purchase prices are stored on your device. In the App Store versions they are also stored in your own iCloud account so that your devices stay in step; only you can read them.

What is sent over the network

To show prices, charts and news, the app requests data for the symbols in your portfolios from the price source you chose in the settings: EODHD, using your own API key, or in the direct-download Mac version optionally Yahoo Finance. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank. These requests go straight from your device to that service and contain the symbols, your IP address as any web request does, and for EODHD your API key. They do not contain quantities or purchase prices. Nothing passes through a server of the developer.

Your API key is stored on your device only, in a private file next to your portfolios. It is not synced and not sent anywhere except to the service it belongs to.

The direct-download Mac version also fetches a small update file from this website to see whether a newer version exists.

AI agents

The optional MCP server gives an assistant that you connect yourself access to your portfolio data on your Mac. What that assistant does with the data is governed by its own privacy terms.
